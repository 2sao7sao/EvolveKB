# Customer Support Refund Policy

Unopened items are eligible for a standard refund when the request is made
within 30 days of purchase and the customer can provide an order number.

Opened items are not automatically eligible after 30 days. If a customer asks
for a refund on an opened item after 45 days, the support agent must escalate
the request to a support manager and finance reviewer before promising any
refund.

Damaged or defective items can be refunded or replaced within 60 days when the
customer provides a photo and the order number.

Support agents must cite policy evidence before giving a refund decision. If the
policy evidence is missing, ambiguous, or conflicting, the agent must return
`needs_review`.
